FAQ

Simple answers before you start

Everything you need to know about using SmartMeal.

What do I get for free?

You can create an account, generate a 7-day meal plan, and use the grocery checklist without paying.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium adds nutrition targets, ingredient-reuse preferences, saved weekly plans, meal favorites, and meal substitutions.

How does billing work?

Premium is billed monthly through Stripe. Subscription management is provided through the secure Stripe billing portal.

Can I cancel later?

Yes. Premium members can open Manage subscription from the planner to access the Stripe billing portal and manage their subscription.

Is the Family plan available?

The Family plan is being finished before launch so its shared household features are ready. It is not available for purchase yet.
