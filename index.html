Good first CRM

HubSpot

Customer management, sales follow-up, forms, and marketing tools.

  • Useful for tracking leads and customer conversations
  • Free tools are available
  • Can grow with a business over time

Best for: small businesses that need one place to organize customers and follow-ups.

Website & store

Shopify

A platform for creating an online store and selling products.

  • Storefront and checkout in one platform
  • Useful when selling products online is the main goal
  • May be more than you need for a simple service website

Best for: businesses planning to sell products online.

Design

Canva

Design software for social posts, flyers, presentations, and simple marketing assets.

  • Easy starting point for non-designers
  • Many ready-made templates
  • Free and paid options

Best for: businesses that need regular visual content without hiring a designer.
