How the math works

A transparent starting formula.

Cost base = labor + travel + supplies + overhead. Target margin is then applied to that cost base, add-ons are added, and any discount is removed. You can change every input to match your own business model.

This calculator intentionally does not claim that the result is the “correct” price for your market. It is a starting point you should validate against real costs and local demand.
